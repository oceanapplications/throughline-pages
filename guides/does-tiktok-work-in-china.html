Does TikTok work in China?

travelersvpn.com guides

No, and it's not just the firewall. TikTok has never operated in mainland China; the Chinese app is Douyin, a separate product with separate accounts. TikTok also checks which carrier your SIM belongs to, not only your IP address, so a VPN by itself can leave you with an empty feed if a mainland SIM is in your phone. It works normally in Hong Kong and Macau. The reliable setup is a foreign SIM or eSIM plus a VPN, all installed before you arrive.

What actually happens when you try

The app opens on an empty For You page, or shows "No internet connection" while every other app is fine. Two things are happening. The Great Firewall drops TikTok's traffic, as it does with Instagram. On top of that, TikTok itself reads the carrier of the SIM in your phone, and if it sees China Mobile or China Unicom it treats you as a mainland user and serves nothing, even over a VPN. That second check is what surprises people who arrive with a working VPN and still see a blank feed.

Douyin, the domestic version, is a different app with a different account system. You can install it out of curiosity, but your TikTok followers, videos, and drafts aren't there.

The setup that actually works

  • Don't put a mainland SIM in the phone. Use your home SIM on roaming, or a travel eSIM from a non-Chinese provider. If you must have a local SIM, keep the foreign line active too, or turn the Chinese line off when you open TikTok.
  • Add a VPN for wifi. On hotel or cafe wifi the firewall is in the way, and a VPN gets you past it. With a foreign SIM in the phone, TikTok's carrier check passes and the feed loads.
  • A roaming eSIM alone works on cellular: the data exits through Hong Kong or Singapore and the carrier is foreign, so TikTok is happy with no VPN. It just quits helping the moment you join wifi.

Frequently asked

Can I use TikTok in China with a VPN?

Only if the phone's SIM is foreign. TikTok checks the SIM's carrier as well as your IP, so a VPN with a China Mobile or China Unicom SIM installed usually shows an empty feed. Foreign SIM or eSIM plus VPN works.

Does TikTok work in Hong Kong or Macau?

Yes, normally, with no VPN. TikTok operates in both, and the Great Firewall applies to mainland China only.

Is Douyin the same as TikTok?

No. Douyin is ByteDance's separate app for mainland China, with its own accounts and content. Your TikTok account, followers, and videos don't exist on Douyin.

Will a travel eSIM make TikTok work in China?

Yes, on cellular. Roaming data exits outside China and the carrier is foreign, so TikTok loads with no VPN. On hotel or cafe wifi you're back behind the firewall and need a VPN.
