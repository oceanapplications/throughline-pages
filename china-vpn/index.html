Common questions.

Does Traveler's VPN actually work in China?

Yes, and the reason is structural rather than a promise: you get a private server with an IP address that has never been shared, so it isn't on the firewall's blocklists the way commercial VPN ranges are. Install and test it before you fly, since nothing can be downloaded from inside.

Do I need to set anything up inside China?

No. Everything happens at home: install, connect once, pick the China profile. After landing, blocked apps work through the tunnel and local apps stay direct automatically.

Will WeChat, Alipay, and Didi slow down?

No. That's the point of the split tunnel: Chinese apps and Apple services stay on direct local routes while only the blocked apps go through your server. Local apps can be up to 10× faster than on a VPN-only app.

Is a VPN legal in China?

It's a genuine grey area: providers need state approval, enforcement has targeted sellers, and there is no public case of a tourist penalized for personal use. Our legality guide lays out the letter and the record honestly.

What does it cost for a two-week trip?

Does it work on hotel wifi?

Yes. Unlike a roaming eSIM, which only helps on cellular, the VPN works on hotel, cafe, and airport wifi, which is where most evenings happen. Many travelers run both.
