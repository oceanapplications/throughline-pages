Common questions.

What makes this different from other VPNs?

Other VPNs are a single on/off switch: the moment you connect, everything routes through the VPN, which is what breaks your banking app, your local maps, and your local payments. Traveler's VPN keeps your local stuff working while you stay connected to home. You get the privacy and home connection without the broken-apps tax.

Do I have to set up my own server?

No. Pick a managed server (or a 7-day travel pass) and we set up a private server for you automatically, in the region you choose. Tap connect and go. If you'd rather run your own, the $2.99 bring-your-own-server unlock connects the app to any Outline, Shadowsocks, WireGuard, or Trojan server you control.

What's the difference between the plans?

The 7-day travel pass ($9.99) is a one-off for a single trip: seven days of a managed server, no auto-renew. The managed server ($14.99/month, starting with a 3-day free trial) is for people who travel often, with a new region available anytime. Bring your own server ($2.99 once) is a one-time unlock for people who already run a server. All three include the smart routing and cover iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Will my banking app still work?

Yes. That's the whole point. Your bank sees a normal connection from where you actually are, so it doesn't lock you out or trigger a fraud check.

Is it really faster than other VPNs?

Yes. Local apps can be up to 10× faster than on a VPN-only app, because local websites, maps, video calls, and streaming don't get rerouted around the world. Only the traffic that actually needs to come from home takes the longer path.

Does it work on Mac?

Yes. There's a native menu-bar Mac app, and your purchase covers iPhone, iPad, and Mac together. Buy or subscribe once and use it everywhere at no extra cost, with your settings synced across your Apple devices.

Do I need to make an account?

No. There's no signup, no email, and no password. Your purchases are tied to your Apple ID behind the scenes, so the app and your managed server just work across your devices. The managed server and travel pass are paid through the App Store; the bring-your-own option is a one-time purchase with nothing recurring.

Can I cancel?

Anytime. The managed subscription cancels like any other App Store subscription, in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. The travel pass never auto-renews, so there's nothing to cancel. The bring-your-own unlock is a one-time purchase.
