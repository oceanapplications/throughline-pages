Common questions.

Do I really need a VPN in Japan?

Not for censorship; Japan blocks nothing. You want one only if you bank or trade from your phone (fraud models flag Japanese IPs) or want your home streaming libraries. If neither applies, skip it.

Will it break Suica or PayPay?

No. The Japan profile keeps Suica, PayPay, JR, and Japanese sites on direct Japanese routes. Only home-country services go through the tunnel. A regular full-tunnel VPN is what breaks the ticket gate.

Why does my bank lock me out in Japan?

Fraud detection: an unfamiliar Japanese IP plus yen charges. Logging in through a private home-country server, the same clean IP every day, avoids the trigger.

Does Hulu work through it?

US Hulu and Peacock play through a private US server, because its IP isn't on their shared-VPN blocklists. Hulu Japan remains a separate service your US account can't use.

Is a VPN legal in Japan?

Yes, completely, for any purpose.
